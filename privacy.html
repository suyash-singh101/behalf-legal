This Privacy Policy explains how Kairos Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Behalf — AI Call Screener mobile application ("Behalf" or the "App").

1. Your Number, Your Control

Your mobile number always belongs to you. Behalf never silently changes your carrier or call-forwarding settings. Call screening only occurs when you explicitly confirm and enable carrier call forwarding on your device, and you can disable forwarding at any time with a single dialer code.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to operate Behalf's call-screening features:

  • Phone Number: Used for one-time password (OTP) authentication, account identification, and routing forwarded calls to your personal assistant.
  • Display Name (Optional): Used so your AI assistant can greet callers politely on your behalf (e.g., "Hi, I'm helping screen calls for [Name]").
  • Forwarded Call Audio, Transcripts & Summaries: Collected only when an incoming call is forwarded to Behalf. We process caller audio in real time to transcribe the conversation, ask who is calling and why, and generate a summary for you.
  • App Preferences & Triage Data: Carrier selection, forwarding status, custom greeting settings, blocked caller numbers, and callback reminders you create inside the App.
  • Subscription & Billing Metadata: Plan status (Free Trial or Behalf Pro) and payment confirmation records processed through Google Play Billing or authorized payment processors. We do not store raw credit/debit card numbers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account via OTP and maintain your active session.
  • Answer, transcribe, and summarize forwarded unknown calls in English and Hindi/Hinglish.
  • Provide live call controls, quick replies, triage actions, and spam/blocked-caller filtering.
  • Manage your Free Trial and Behalf Pro subscription status.
  • Diagnose technical issues, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable Indian law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

4. Data Sharing & Advertising

  • No Ads & No Data Sales: Behalf contains no third-party advertisements. We never sell your personal data or share your call data with third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • Service Providers (Processors): To deliver real-time voice call screening, forwarded call audio and text are processed by our contracted telephony and speech/AI infrastructure providers strictly under data-processing safeguards and solely to provide the App's functionality.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or lawful government request.

5. Security & Encryption

All data transmitted between the Behalf App and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device, and access to backend data is restricted using least-privilege controls.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data and call summaries only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can delete individual call records or request complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

For step-by-step instructions, see our Account & Data Deletion page.

7. Children's Privacy

Behalf is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at suyashsngh250@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this page and notify you inside the App where appropriate.
