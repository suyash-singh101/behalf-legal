Account & Data Deletion

Behalf — AI Call Screener (com.heybehalf.app) · Kairos Technologies

You can permanently delete your Behalf — AI Call Screener account (published by Kairos Technologies) and all associated personal data at any time using either of the methods below.

Option 1: Delete Directly in the Behalf App (Fastest)

  1. Open Behalf — AI Call Screener on your phone and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account is immediately deactivated and queued for permanent purge.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email (Without App Access)

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in:

  1. Send an email to suyashsngh250@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete Behalf Account".
  2. Include the phone number (with country code, e.g., +91...) registered with your Behalf account.
  3. We will verify ownership of the phone number and process your deletion request within 7 business days.
Important reminder before deleting: If you enabled carrier call forwarding (e.g., Jio *413 or Airtel/Vi ##002#), please dial your carrier's disable code on your phone so future calls ring your device directly.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following data is immediately soft-deleted (inaccessible) and permanently purged from our primary databases and backups within 30 days:

  • Your account profile (registered phone number and display name)
  • Carrier, SIM, greeting, and forwarding preferences
  • Blocked caller lists and callback reminders
  • All screened call transcripts, summaries, and call audio recordings
  • Active authentication tokens and session records

What Data May Be Retained & Retention Period

  • Billing & Tax Records: If you purchased a Behalf Pro subscription, minimal invoice/transaction metadata required under Indian tax and accounting laws may be retained for up to 7 years. These records never contain call audio or transcripts.
  • Fraud / Abuse Logs: Non-content security logs may be retained for up to 90 days to prevent abuse and ensure service integrity.

Partial Data Deletion (Without Deleting Your Account)

You can also delete individual call logs, transcripts, or blocked numbers directly inside the Behalf app at any time without deleting your account, or email suyashsngh250@gmail.com to request deletion of specific call history records.
